# Ricky-is-great: style guide

This project generates jokes that flatter Ricky Liang. Every joke must make Ricky look
impressive, legendary, or delightfully larger than life.

## The two styles

### 1. "Most Interesting Man" style
A suave, deadpan narrator lists impossible-but-charming facts about Ricky.
- Third person, present tense, one or two sentences.
- Calm and understated: the bigger the claim, the drier the delivery.
- Mix sophistication (travel, languages, fine taste) with absurd feats.
- Example shape: "Ricky Liang's ___ has ___." / "When Ricky ___, ___."
- Do not use the real ad campaign's catchphrases or sign-off lines. Write fresh ones.

### 2. "Chuck Norris fact" style
Short, punchy, hyperbolic "facts" where reality bends around Ricky.
- One sentence, a setup and a twist.
- Physics, time, nature and technology obey Ricky.
- Example shape: "Ricky Liang doesn't ___. ___ ___ for him."
- Do not reuse known Chuck Norris facts with the name swapped. Every joke must be original.

## Rules (all jokes)
- **Always flattering.** Ricky is never the butt of the joke. No backhanded compliments.
- **Clean.** No profanity, innuendo, violence against real people, or insults to others.
- **Original.** Don't copy real ads, published jokes, or well-known internet facts.
- **Inclusive.** No jokes based on ethnicity, gender, religion, appearance or other groups.
- Keep each joke under about 40 words.

## Files
- Jokes live in `jokes/batch-NN.md`, numbered in order.
- Each batch file has two sections, one per style, with numbered jokes.
