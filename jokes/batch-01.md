# Batch 01

## "Most Interesting Man" style

1. Ricky Liang once gave a stranger directions in Lisbon. The stranger now runs the city's tourism board.
2. Ricky's handwriting has been framed by three museums, and all he wrote was a grocery list.
3. When Ricky orders "the usual," restaurants he has never visited know exactly what to bring.
4. Ricky Liang doesn't check the weather forecast. The forecast checks in with Ricky.
5. Ricky's houseplants don't need sunlight. They grow toward his good ideas.

## Chuck Norris-fact style

1. Ricky Liang doesn't debug code. The bugs file their own resignation letters.
2. Ricky once finished a marathon so fast the finish line had to jog to keep up.
3. Calendars have a 366th day every four years just to fit in more of Ricky's achievements.
4. Ricky Liang doesn't wait for elevators. Gravity politely brings each floor to him.
5. When Ricky enters a room, Wi-Fi gets one bar stronger out of respect.
